**Joao Moura - Professional Bio**

Joao Moura, also known as Joe Moura, is the Founder and CEO of CrewAI, a leading platform that specializes in multi-agent AI systems designed to automate complex workflows for enterprises. With nearly 20 years of experience in software engineering and AI, Joao has established himself as a prominent figure in the AI industry. 

Before founding CrewAI, Joao served as the Director of AI Engineering at Clearbit, a company acquired by HubSpot, where he contributed significantly to the development of data-driven solutions that leverage artificial intelligence for business growth. His expertise in AI and engineering makes him a valuable asset in the tech community, and he is known for his open-source contributions and public speaking engagements.

Joao's vision for CrewAI is to empower organizations through innovative AI solutions that streamline operations and enhance productivity. Under his leadership, CrewAI has launched several groundbreaking initiatives, including the Multi-Agentic Platform and CrewAI Enterprise, aimed at Fortune 500 companies. The platform enables businesses to create fleets of AI agents that can work collaboratively to automate various tasks across different sectors.
